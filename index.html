About Us

Agasthiyar yoga centre

Agasthiyar Yoga Centre is a dedicated wellness establishment located in Kalikkanaicken Palayam, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Named after the revered sage Agasthiyar — one of the greatest proponents of yoga, Siddha medicine, and holistic living — our centre carries forward a tradition of authentic yogic practices tailored for modern lifestyles. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced practitioner, we welcome you to a serene space designed for healing, growth, and inner peace.

Conveniently open five days a week from early morning through the evening, we accommodate working professionals, students, homemakers, and seniors alike. Our experienced instructors guide students through postures, breathing techniques, and meditation practices that promote physical strength, mental clarity, and emotional well-being. Join our growing community and take the first step toward a healthier, more balanced life at Agasthiyar Yoga Centre.

  • Open Monday to Friday from 5:00 AM to 6:30 PM with flexible morning and evening batches
  • Located conveniently on Narasipuram Road, Coimbatore — easily accessible from surrounding areas
  • Rooted in the tradition of Sage Agasthiyar with authentic, holistic yoga and wellness practices
